# The Meanings of CI, BI, VI, and MI

All marketing concepts can ultimately be categorized as subsets of the Corporate Identity (CI) system.

## CI (Corporate Identity)
- CI stands for "Corporate Identity"
- It is a systematic corporate branding strategy encompassing the company's business philosophy and behavioral standards
- Comprised of three subsystems: MI (Mind Identity), BI (Behavior Identity), and VI (Visual Identity)

## MI (Mind Identity)
- MI is the core component of CI
- Refers to "Mind Identity"
- Defines the company's core values and philosophy, guiding the overall direction and tone of CI design

## BI (Behavior Identity)
- BI refers to "Behavior Identity"
- Within the corporate identity system, it focuses on translating the philosophy into practice
- Drives daily operations through specific behavioral standards, ensuring all employees understand and implement the company's core philosophy
- BI also refers to "Business Intelligence," including tools for data analysis, visualization, and market research analysis

## VI (Visual Identity)
- VI stands for "Visual Identity"
- It forms the foundation and a key component of corporate image development
- Communicates the company's philosophy through visual design, including corporate logos, symbols, standard fonts, standard colors, imagery, and all other visual elements
